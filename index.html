Who We Are

Our Mission

Started in January 2020 as a response to the COVID-19 crisis, the Homeless Child & Widows Foundation is a registered Kenyan NGO (Reg: D7UNKJV) dedicated to relieving poverty and improving access to quality education and healthcare.

We operate across Mombasa, Kilifi, Nyandarua, Nairobi, and Lamu, running three distinct wings: Children's Rescue, Women's Empowerment, and Youth Empowerment.

  • Rescue and shelter homeless, orphaned, and vulnerable children
  • Empower widows with skills, microfinance, and business support
  • Provide food, clothing, and medical care to those in extreme poverty
  • Pay school fees and provide learning materials for needy students
What We Do

Our Empowerment Wings

All three categories are registered separately under the umbrella of the main foundation.

Children's Rescue Wing

Providing immediate intervention, safe shelter, food, and education for homeless, orphaned, and disabled children.

Women's Empowerment Wing

Supporting widows through skills training, microfinance programs, and business startups to achieve financial independence.

Youth Empowerment Wing

Providing education support, grants, and equipment for young people to establish sustainable livelihoods.

Flagship Project

Maweni Children Rescue Home

Located in Mombasa, the Maweni Rescue Home provides a safe haven for street orphans and vulnerable children who have nowhere else to call home. Here, they receive shelter from the cold nights and heavy rains, hot meals, and access to education.

We are currently renting a large house to serve these children. Our long-term goal is to buy land and build a permanent, self-sustaining rescue home rather than spending funds on rent.
