About Us

The organization was later registered by the Kenya Government as a COMMUNITY BASED ORGANIZATION (CBO) whereby we were issued with a varied certificate to serve the community.

The organization later branched into three main categories in one way of reaching the needy people in the community as following: Orphans, homeless, disabled and vulnerable Children's wing; Women empowerment wing; Youth empowerment wing.

All the three categories are registered separately and have their varied genuine certificates but all under one umbrella of the main foundation. All the certificates are much varied up to date. We support these needy people by donating food, clothes, medical care, education and education materials and shelter. For the categories who have projects we give them grand money to boost their projects.

Our staffs are highly trained starting from our chairman who holds a diploma certificate in this field. Other staffs are social workers who have fronted to work hard for the poor people mostly the needy children. The Kenya Government officials assists us in case of advises in problem solving. We have children in primary schools, secondary schools and colleges.

The Problems We Are Solving

"Most of the community especially in the rural areas moves half naked. This goes to the children and adults too. These people need clothes to hide their nakedness."

"The poor people in the community goes around with empty stomachs. Most of them gets only one small meal per day. They are starving for food and clean water."

"Medical and health attention especially AID, HIV and Cancer complication patients is a great problem as they can't afford the price of medicine when they fall sick."

"Many children from the poor families do not attend schools. The family cannot afford to pay for the school fees and the education materials."

Maweni Children Rescue Home

We have street orphans and vulnerable children who live in the street eating from the garbage and waste from the markets areas. They have nowhere they can call HOME. To try and solve this big problem in Mombasa, we have rented a big house for these poor people to live in and have free food etc. We call this home MAWENI CHILDREN RESCUE HOME.

We are preparing to rent more and more houses in the country for the poor people to live in. If we gets enough funds we buy a land and build a rescue home instead of using a most of money renting rooms for the poor children.
