Our 5 Key Missions

We are committed to transforming lives through these core focus areas

Widow Empowerment

Our Empowerment Wings

Dual focus on homeless children's welfare and widow empowerment

Homeless Children's Rescue Wing

Focused on rescue, rehabilitation, education, and healthcare for homeless children. Our children's homes provide safe havens and nurturing environments for those who have been abandoned or orphaned.

Widows' Empowerment Wing

Empowering widows through skills training, entrepreneurship support, and advocacy. We help widows become financially independent and community leaders, breaking cycles of poverty.

Family Support Wing

Working with entire families to create sustainable livelihoods, ensuring children can remain with their families whenever possible through economic empowerment and support systems.

Special Focus: Widow Empowerment

In Kenya, widows often face immense challenges including loss of property, social exclusion, and economic hardship. Our Widow Empowerment Program provides:

  • Skills training in tailoring, agriculture, and small business management
  • Microfinance loans and business startup kits
  • Legal support for property and inheritance rights
  • Counseling and peer support groups
  • Educational support for their children

Through this program, widows regain their dignity, achieve financial independence, and become pillars of their communities.
